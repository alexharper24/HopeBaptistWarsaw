Our Partnership with Bearing Precious Seed

Hope Baptist Church is privileged to partner with Bearing Precious Seed in the work of getting Scripture into the hands of people across the globe. This ministry is one of the ways our church family directly participates in the Great Commission, not just through giving, but through hands-on work right here in Warsaw.

Together, we help produce New Testaments, Gospel of John booklets, and John & Romans booklets in various languages. These Scriptures are then sent to missionaries and churches around the world who distribute them in their communities.

What We Do

Our church handles the assembly side of Scripture production. This is real, hands-on ministry that people of all ages can participate in:

Collating
Gathering and organizing printed pages in the correct order
Stapling & Binding
Assembling booklets with staples or glue binding
Cutting & Trimming
Trimming assembled Scriptures to their finished size
Raising Support
Funding the materials and shipping costs for each project

Other churches and ministries within the Bearing Precious Seed network handle the printing and overseas shipping. Our role is the assembly work and financial support that makes each project possible.

What We Produce

Each project focuses on a specific language and Scripture portion. The materials we assemble include:

New Testaments Gospel of John John & Romans Various Languages

Every booklet that leaves our church represents an opportunity for someone on the other side of the world to read the Word of God, many for the very first time.
