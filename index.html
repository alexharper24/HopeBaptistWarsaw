Welcome Home

Welcome to Hope Baptist Church

We are a King James Bible believing Baptist church on the outskirts of Winona Lake and Warsaw, Indiana. You will find solid preaching from God's Word, Godly traditional worship, and a church family that genuinely cares. Whether you are new to faith or have walked with Christ for years, we would love to meet you.
