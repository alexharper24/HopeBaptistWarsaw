Titus Women

A ministry for the women of Hope Baptist Church.

“That they may teach the young women to be sober, to love their husbands, to love their children” (Titus 2:4)
← All Ministries
Launching October 2026

Standing. Strengthening. Serving. For His Glory.

Titus Women is a new ministry for the women of Hope Baptist Church, built on the pattern of Titus 2:3-5. It is a place for women of every age to grow in the Word, encourage one another, and serve together, older women teaching and younger women learning, all for the glory of God.

Teaching Investing Training Serving Upholding

“...the effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much.” (James 5:16)

What to Expect

Titus Women officially begins in October 2026. Meeting times and a full schedule will be announced soon. In the meantime, if you would like to be part of it or simply want to learn more, we would love to hear from you.

Interested in Titus Women? Reach out and we will keep you posted.
